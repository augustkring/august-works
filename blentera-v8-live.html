DESIGN PRINCIPLES — NOT CERTIFICATION CLAIMS ↙
THE CONTROL MODEL

Every agent acts inside a boundary.

BLENTERA's product design separates instructions from permissions and review. More autonomy should not mean less clarity about who can do what.

01

Access is scoped.

Roles, data permissions and available tools determine what an agent is allowed to access or operate.

⛓
02

Consequential actions need approval.

People remain responsible for decisions that affect money, sensitive data or irreversible changes.

◎
03

Work leaves evidence.

Outputs, decisions and relevant context can be reviewed before they are reused as company knowledge.
