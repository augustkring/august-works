Built for capability that stays.

BLENTERA was founded by August Kring to help companies use AI without losing knowledge, standards or control every time technology changes.

The platform is being qualified for hosted operations and initial customer use. The focus is repeatable work and measured value, not agent counts.

August Works is a separate implementation partner, not BLENTERA. Official company contact and legal disclosures are being finalized.

Controls belong to the work.

Responsibility is clearest when it's attached to the task and its consequences.

Know who is responsible.

Give tasks a named owner and assign AI work to clear roles.

Limit access.

Scope agents to the data and actions they need for the task.

Review important actions.

Require approval before designated sensitive or external changes.

Keep a record.

Attach decisions, evidence and corrections to the work so people can inspect what happened.
