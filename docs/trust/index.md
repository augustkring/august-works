Blentera's general contact is [hello@blentera.com](mailto:hello@blentera.com).
Use [security@blentera.com](mailto:security@blentera.com) for security concerns
and [privacy@blentera.com](mailto:privacy@blentera.com) for privacy requests.

This overview was reviewed against product documentation on **27 October 2026**.
It is not a deployment audit, a legal agreement or a certification. For a
procurement or data-processing decision, request current evidence for the
specific deployment, provider and processing purpose you intend to use.

## Security and vulnerability disclosure

Company access is governed by current membership, permissions and applicable
approval policies. A Co-worker's identity, a tool description or a connection
label does not itself grant access. Runtime and hosting qualifications have
specific scopes; do not infer the same controls for every runtime.

For a vulnerability, follow the [coordinated disclosure policy](/trust/vulnerability-disclosure).
The application publishes its security contact at
[/.well-known/security.txt](https://app.blentera.com/.well-known/security.txt).
The contact file has an explicit expiry; its publication is separate from proof
that a mailbox or incident-response process has been operationally tested.

## Privacy, regions and subprocessors

Hosting region, model processing, connected Apps, telemetry, Support access and
backup storage are separate processing questions. Hosting in one region does
not establish that every provider processes all data in that region.

This overview does not publish an approved deployment-specific region or
subprocessor list. Request the current Privacy Notice, DPA, subprocessor list
and hosting/backup statement from [privacy@blentera.com](mailto:privacy@blentera.com)
before relying on a particular processing arrangement. A privacy disclosure is
not consent to optional sharing or marketing.

## Responsible AI

Co-workers operate within the applicable Human responsibility, permissions,
budget, approval and source-access controls. Memory and retrieved material are
evidence, not permission to broaden authority. Provider/model eligibility and
runtime qualifications must be checked for the actual work; a fallback must
meet the same applicable processing and authority requirements.

Request the current deployment's AI transparency and governance information
when assessing a particular use case. This page does not claim that citing the
AI Act or another standard establishes compliance.

## Accessibility

Blentera's product work targets accessible keyboard, focus, language and
assistive-technology behavior. Automated checks cover a limited set of
journeys. This page does not claim an independent accessibility audit or
universal WCAG conformance. Report a barrier to
[support@blentera.com](mailto:support@blentera.com), including the affected view,
browser and assistive technology when relevant. Do not include private work
or credentials in the report.

## Portability, deletion and continuity

See [export and deletion](/help/export-and-delete) for the supported account and
company flows. Scope, authority, retained records and any unknown result matter;
a saved request is not proof that every deletion or external effect completed.

Request current backup, restore, business-continuity and recovery evidence for
the deployment you use. This page does not promise a universal recovery time,
recovery point, backup region or retention period.

## Service status

The independent [Blentera status page](https://status.blentera.com) is the place
for published component, incident and scheduled-maintenance communication.
Use its subscription and history controls for provider-managed notifications
and past updates. In-app status is a projection of that communication and does
not overwrite the state of a Task or run. Human Support remains available.

## Assurance documents and certifications

No certification or audit report is listed as current in this overview. This
is not a statement that a report exists or that a certification has been earned.
Ask [hello@blentera.com](mailto:hello@blentera.com?subject=Assurance%20request) for
current approved evidence and its scope, owner, version, review date and expiry.

Depending on the request and available evidence, an assurance pack may include
an audit report, penetration-test summary, continuity/recovery evidence,
security questionnaire, supplier/region statement or architecture overview.
Restricted material may require an NDA and an authorized review. A request does
not itself grant access, and expired or superseded evidence must not be presented
as current.
