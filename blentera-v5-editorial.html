AI can plan, delegate and do work. That makes roles, access, approvals and evidence more important — not less.

Operating principles 01
01

Scope before autonomy.

Access and authority should be explicit. An agent receives only the permissions necessary for its approved work.

02

Decisions have owners.

Consequential actions such as spending, publishing, access changes and production deployments require a designated human decision maker.

03

Results need evidence.

Work should be reviewable: what was asked, who did it, what changed and why the result was accepted.

04

Learning is reviewed.

Useful outcomes can become reusable company knowledge — after validation, not through uncontrolled memory accumulation.
