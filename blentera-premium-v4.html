    A BASIC RULE

    The model proposes.
    The company decides.

    Human judgment owns direction, accountability and consequential approvals. The system constrains actions through identity, scope, permissions and verifiable outcomes — not just instructions inside an AI prompt.

    Controls that travel
    with the work.

    The goal is useful autonomy with boundaries people can understand, inspect and change.

    01

    Clear identity & scope

    Every actor — person, agent or system — needs a known role, purpose and access boundary.

    ⌘
    02

    Approvals before high-impact action

    Escalate spending, production changes and destructive operations to authorised human decision-makers.

    ◇
    03

    Evidence over confidence

    Track work states, decisions and postconditions. Verify meaningful outcomes and preserve audit evidence.

    ≡
    04

    Portable, company-owned capability

    Keep company context and reusable procedures independent of a particular agent, model or runtime.
