# Blentera Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability

Report vulnerabilities privately to [security@blentera.com](mailto:security@blentera.com).
Include the affected version, impact and reproducible steps using synthetic data.
Keep credentials and private customer content out of the report.

Do not open public issues for security vulnerabilities.

Product help belongs at [support@blentera.com](mailto:support@blentera.com).
Privacy and data-subject requests belong at
[privacy@blentera.com](mailto:privacy@blentera.com).
