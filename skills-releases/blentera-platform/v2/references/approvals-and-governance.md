# Approvals and governance

A proposal is not approval. Approval binds exact parameters, destination, principal, company, version, consequence and expiry through canonical state. Free-text agreement or a channel reaction cannot replace the required structured approval. Never self-approve an authority expansion. Recheck current authority and the still-valid action binding immediately before execution. Changed parameters, revoked membership, expired consent, consumed approval or stale version require authoritative reconciliation; an old approval cannot authorize a new action. Use the same idempotency identity when checking an unknown result. Inspect the durable executed, declined, failed or pending receipt rather than inferring success from a delivered notification. Discover current approval modes and contracts.
