# Errors, retries and recovery

DENIED: stop; do not bypass through another tool, browser or identity. CONFLICT/STALE: read current state and reconcile before proposing another action. TIMEOUT/UNKNOWN: inspect the durable operation and external outcome before retry; do not refund liability or repeat an effect without evidence. PARTIAL EFFECT: reconcile or use an explicitly authorized compensation. REVOKED/PAUSED: fence further work and leave a truthful durable waiting state. DEPENDENCY UNAVAILABLE: report the affected capability as degraded without inventing a replacement. A Stop request is distinct from confirmed workload termination. Preserve stable work and idempotency identity; a resumed attempt uses current authority and the original bounded work identity. Discover the current recovery action and verify its receipt.
