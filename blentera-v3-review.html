THE PRINCIPLE

The model proposes.
The company decides.

An AI organisation needs more than job titles. It needs explicit permissions, owners, reviewable work, clear handoffs, measured outcomes and ways to recover when something goes wrong.

Our aim is a company that becomes more capable without becoming dependent on one person, one agent or one model provider.

GOVERNANCE

Useful autonomy.
Explicit authority.

AI can take work forward without being allowed to decide its own limits. BLENTERA's control model separates agent suggestions from permissions, review and irreversible actions.

A SIMPLE EXAMPLE

Let agents work.
Keep sensitive decisions human.

The important distinction isn't whether a task was generated by AI. It's who authorised it, what the agent was allowed to do and whether the result can be verified.

Example agent permissionsSCOPED
Read approved company contextAllowed
Prepare a draftAllowed
Publish externallyApproval required
Make a paymentApproval required
Change access policyNot delegated
Illustrative policy only. A live implementation must enforce authorisation server-side.

Control is part of the operating model.

01 / Know who's acting

Named human and agent identities, scoped authority and resource boundaries.

02 / Make decisions visible

Clear approvals for consequential work, with context and evidence available to the reviewer.

03 / Check what happened

Verification, audit trails, reliable job state and appropriate escalation after failure.

04 / Stay portable

Company-owned context, export and replaceable model and runtime providers.

Controls are not a compliance certificate.

BLENTERA is designed to support responsible AI operation. Whether an organisation complies with applicable obligations depends on its specific use case, deployment, processing and independent verification.
