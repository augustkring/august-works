# V9 private history and feedback acknowledgement fences

Explicit retry after a failed Native session rechecks the original session owner
before enabling a fresh private read. Pending or failed session recovery hides
history and operator controls, coalesces duplicate session retries and never
replays a feedback write. Operator entry and the maintenance route also expose
this recovery when no private child has mounted. The extended focused check is
pending; the earlier 123 passing checks qualify the preceding source only.

Scope: V9 §§9.2, 30, 38, 85.15–85.18 and 85.24. Customer display is C2;
session isolation and protected operator admission are C3. The attached privacy,
security, UX and frontend playbooks are reference guidelines. This checkpoint
does not close the full V9 backlog or authorize a release.

The original auth-session query, Customer Feedback service, operator allowlist,
company membership and maintenance installation remain the authority owners.
The client subscribes to the original session cache and checks its full Native
response before and after reads or writes. Pending, invalidated, malformed or
changed sessions hide private history. A late response cannot populate a fresh
session's view. Private query keys include the current read epoch, use no-store
transport, consume the original abort signal and retain zero inactive lifetime.
No new token, grant, storage inventory or background mutation is introduced.

Feedback history remains available after new submissions are disabled, subject
to the original service's current ownership checks. Operator triage retains its
separate Native operator admission and may select an explicitly entered company;
company administrator status does not establish platform operator access.
Maintenance history retains current feature, company and resource admission.
Its historical receipts confer no repair, retry or activation authority.

During a same-principal recheck, an unsent reply or frozen unknown request stays
in its mounted scoped component and disappears from the DOM. A different
principal or company remounts that component. Writes check original Native read
state at dispatch and acknowledgement boundaries. Unknown acknowledgement keeps
the exact original UUID and body; a later validation refusal does not prove that
an earlier unknown attempt had no effect. Only a currently confirmed original
receipt clears a frozen request. No automatic replay is added.

Triage acknowledgement validates the original strict customer receipt and its
company/feedback IDs. Source errors use fixed product text. The triage page has a
labelled section within the existing layout landmark and focuses its heading
without disturbing a Native dialog.

Verification pending: focused tests of original Native identity observation,

Rollback restores the previous presentation code while preserving all Native
feedback, events, request receipts and installation history. It does not reset
unknown requests, enable output export or change operator permission policy.
