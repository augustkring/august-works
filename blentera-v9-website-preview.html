A goal moves through the organisation.

Here's how a market-entry request could move from a human decision to specialist work and a reusable outcome.

Human ownerSet the objective

“Prepare a plan for entering Germany.”

Chief of StaffDivide the work

Route the research, budget and sales questions to the right functions.

Specialist teamsPrepare the evidence

Growth analyses demand. Finance models costs. Revenue outlines outreach.

Human approvalMake the call

Review trade-offs and approve the plan before external action.

What stays: a reviewed checklist and the decisions behind it, available to the next person or agent.
