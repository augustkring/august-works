01 / THE PROBLEM

AI makes individuals faster.
That doesn’t automatically
make a company stronger.

Every new assistant, agent or vendor can mean another disconnected source of knowledge. Prompts are rewritten. Context is re-explained. Someone leaves and the working method leaves with them.

BLENTERA is designed around a different unit of value: the company’s reusable operating capability.
