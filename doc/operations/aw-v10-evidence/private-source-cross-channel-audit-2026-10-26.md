## Separate provider qualification

All cited Slack/Teams transport cases use mocked APIs or configured test runtimes. Software integration success cannot certify delivery into a real Slack workspace or Teams tenant, real provider ACL behavior, actual object storage, or live credential revocation. Those require a separate controlled qualification against test tenants and real storage with test identities. Keep those results separate from CI software coverage and do not claim them from these tests.
