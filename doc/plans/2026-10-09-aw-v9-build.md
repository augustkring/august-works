## Baseline and validation design

Measure real baseline route latency, TTFO, task success, attention precision and
critical recall, recovery and customer effort on the same representative cohort
and environment as the challenger. Do not manufacture baseline values from the
brief. The coverage manifest tracks implementation and evidence separately.
Exploratory trials identify failure modes; freeze confirmatory population,
journeys, thresholds, minimum sample, exclusions and PASS/FAIL/INCONCLUSIVE
rules before collecting confirmatory evidence. High-consequence failures are
assessed separately from averages. Exact source/config/artifact identity is
required for release evidence. No confirmatory result or hosted baseline exists
at this initial checkpoint.
